## 提示词

Create a minimal Windows desktop app icon with real transparent alpha background. Subject: EXACTLY THREE freestanding solid colored rectangular PANES, with completely empty transparent gaps between them. There is NO enclosing shape, NO backing panel, NO frame, NO border around the group, NO badge, NO baseplate, NO outer square. The three panels themselves ARE the entire icon. Composition: one tall vertical blue pane on the left; two identical square panes stacked at right, one sky blue above, one deeper azure below. Retain a simple left-tall/right-double grouping structure. All three are independent separated silhouettes on transparency. Design grid 48x48: tall left pane x=5..23,y=7..41; upper right square x=27..43,y=5..21; lower right square x=27..43,y=25..41. Thus slight step at the top gives distinctive overall silhouette, right squares truly equal width and height, bottom edges aligned, consistent 4-unit negative-space gutters. Modest 2-unit corner radii, NOT pills or large squircle corners. Elegant Microsoft Windows Fluent app-icon construction: front-facing flat colored planes, subtle two-stop gradients, thin darker blue bottom-edge slivers suggesting only slight depth; no shine, glass, puffy bevels, highlights, external shadows, perspective, symbols, text or decorations. Use coordinated blue values: left medium cobalt #2563EB to #1D4ED8, upper right lighter sky #38BDF8 to #0EA5E9, lower right azure #3B82F6 to #2563EB. Not white panels inside a blue background. Three standalone colored panes and NOTHING behind them. No four-pane Windows logo. Transparent background and transparent gutters, single finished icon asset, square canvas with optically centered composition. Simplicity, modest rounding, clean silhouettes, generous clear separation and balanced proportions are paramount.
